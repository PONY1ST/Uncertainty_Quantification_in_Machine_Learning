\section*{Exercise 2}

Consider split conformal prediction with $\mathcal{Y} = \mathbb{R}^d$
and a given model $\hat{f}: \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}^d$
whose coordinates are $\hat{f}(x) = (\hat{f}_1(x), \ldots, \hat{f}_d(x))$.
Construct a score function $s(x,y)$
that would yield intervals of the form 

\begin{equation*}
    \mathcal{C}(x) =  \prod_{j=1}^{d} [\hat{f}_j(x) - c, \hat{f}_j(x) + c]
\end{equation*}
for a $c > 0$.

\paragraph{Solution}
Put 

\begin{equation*}
  s(x,y) = \max_{j=1,\ldots,d} \abs{\hat{f}_j(x)-y_j}.
\end{equation*}
Then, with $\hat{q}$
being a quantile of the scores on the validation set
and $\mathcal{C}(x) = \{y \in \mathcal{Y} \mid s(x,y) \leqslant \hat{q}\}$,
we will have the desired interval with $c = \hat{q}$.
