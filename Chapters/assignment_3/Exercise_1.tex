\section*{Exercise 1}

Consider split conformal prediction with $\mathcal{Y} = \mathbb{R}$
and a given model $\hat{f}: \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$.
Construct a score function $s(x,y)$
that would yield intervals of the form $\mathcal{C}(x) 
= [\hat{f}(x) - \gamma, \hat{f}(x) + 2 \gamma]$ for some $\gamma > 0$.

\paragraph{Solution}
Put 

\begin{equation*}
  s(x,y) = 
  \begin{cases}
    2 \abs{\hat{f}(x)-y} \tab[5cm] & y < \hat{f}(x) \\
    \abs{\hat{f}(x)-y} \tab[5.1cm] & y \geqslant \hat{f}(x).
  \end{cases}
\end{equation*}
Then, with $\hat{q}$
being a quantile of the scores on the validation set
and $\mathcal{C}(x) = \{y \in \mathcal{Y} \mid s(x,y) \leqslant \hat{q}\}$,
we will have the desired interval with $\gamma = \frac{\hat{q}}{2}$.
